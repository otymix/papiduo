Help & contact

PapiDuo: 11 games for two on one screen, for kids aged 4-10 and their parents.

How to play

  • Lay the iPhone or iPad flat between you: each player plays on their side, and the top half of the screen faces the player opposite.
  • In every game, the ❓ button in the menu explains the rules in a few lines.
  • To play alone, choose “Against the computer” (3 levels). “Guess My Drawing” is for two players only.
  • During a game, the ☰ button lets you pause, resume or go back home.
  • On the home screen, the “What does your child want to practise?” tabs filter games by skill, and the 🇫🇷 🇬🇧 🇪🇸 flags change the language.

FAQ

I can't hear the voice. Check the volume and that silent mode is off. In some games, the voice can be turned off in the menu.

My child keeps losing against me. Use the head starts: “Blue waits before buzzing” (Sound Hunt), “Head start for Red” (Number Hide & Seek), boxes of different sizes (Shut the Box), “Together” mode (Magic Shapes)…

Do I need an internet connection? No. PapiDuo works completely offline, with no ads and no account.

How do I delete saved drawings or monsters? They are stored only on the device; deleting the app erases them.
