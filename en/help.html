Help & contact

PapiDuo: 30 games for two on one screen, for kids aged 4-12 and their parents.

How to play

  • Lay the phone or tablet flat between you: each player plays on their side, and the top half of the screen faces the player opposite.
  • On the first game, a guide shows what to tap step by step; afterwards, “🎓 How to play?” and the ❓ button in the menu remind you.
  • To play alone, choose “Against the computer” (3 levels). “Guess My Drawing” is for two players only.
  • During a game, the ☰ button lets you pause, resume or go back home.
  • On the home screen, the 🔍 search and the age chips help you find a game, and the ☆ star adds it to your favourites, and the 🇫🇷 🇬🇧 🇪🇸 flags change the language.

Evening Tournament

Tap the 🏆 banner at the top of the home screen, enter the names (2 to 4 players) and the length (7, 15 or 21 evenings). Each evening, tap “Play” for each match: the names replace Red and Blue, and the result is saved automatically. The evening reminder is turned on at the bottom of the tournament page, after a question for parents; turn it off there or in Settings > Notifications > PapiDuo.

FAQ

I can't hear the voice. Check the volume and that silent mode is off. In some games, the voice can be turned off in the menu.

My child keeps losing against me. Use the head starts: “Blue waits before buzzing” (Sound Hunt), “Head start for Red” (Number Hide & Seek), boxes of different sizes (Shut the Box), “Together” mode (Magic Shapes)…

Do I need an internet connection? No. PapiDuo works completely offline, with no ads and no account.

How do I delete saved drawings or monsters? They are stored only on the device; deleting the app erases them.
