Privacy Policy

PapiDuo app for iPhone and iPad · Last updated: November 11, 2026

PapiDuo does not collect any personal data. None at all.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-up: no name, age or email address is ever requested.
  • No internet connection: the app works completely offline.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no advertising identifier, no cross-app tracking.
  • No in-app purchases. No links to external websites inside the app.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

What stays on your device

For convenience, the app stores only on your device: your settings (language, voice, skills filter), the drawings found in “Guess My Drawing”, the monsters in the Monster Collection, the Magic Shapes album and your records. This information never leaves the device and is erased if you delete the app.

Evening Tournament and reminder

The names entered for the Evening Tournament and the scores stay only on the device. If a parent turns on the evening reminder, the app schedules a local notification on the device: no notification goes through a server and nothing is sent.

Voice

Some instructions are read aloud by the speech synthesis built into iOS, directly on the device. The microphone is never used and no sound is recorded.

Children

PapiDuo is designed to be used by a child together with an adult. Since no data is collected, no data about children is collected (GDPR, COPPA).

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. GitHub may keep technical logs (IP address) for the security of its service: see GitHub's privacy statement.
