Privacy Policy

PapiDuo app for iPhone, iPad and Android · Last updated: November 25, 2026

PapiDuo does not collect any personal data. None at all.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-up: no name, age or email address is ever requested.
  • No internet connection: the app works completely offline.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no advertising identifier, no cross-app tracking.
  • No in-app purchases. No links to external websites inside the app.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

What stays on your device

For convenience, the app stores only on your device: your settings (language, voice, skills filter), the drawings found in “Guess My Drawing”, the monsters in the Monster Collection, the Magic Shapes album and your records. This information never leaves the device and is erased if you delete the app.

Welcome flow preferences

On first launch, a short flow asks the children's age and what they like to practise, to suggest suitable games. These answers stay only on the device: they are never sent or shared, and you can redo them at any time.

Names, scores and reminder

The names and ages entered ("The family", "Who's playing?", Evening Tournament) and game scores stay only on the device: they are never sent, to us or anyone else, and are deleted with the app. They are optional: you can play without them. If a parent turns on the evening reminder, the app schedules a local notification on the device: no notification goes through a server and nothing is sent.

Voice and microphone

Some instructions are read aloud by the speech synthesis built into your phone or tablet, directly on the device (the voice can be chosen in “🔊 Voice” on the home screen). The microphone is used only in The Sound Factory, and only if a parent turns it on: the sound is analysed instantly, on the device, to move the game forward. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent.

Children

PapiDuo is designed to be used by a child together with an adult. Since no data is collected, no data about children is collected (GDPR, COPPA).

This website
