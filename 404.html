FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes: all 30 games, with no ads and no purchases.

Do I need internet or an account?

No. PapiDuo works completely offline, with no sign-up. No data is collected.

What age is it for?

Ages 4 to 12, with an adult or another child. Each player picks their level: an older and a younger child can play together.

What if we play alone?

Almost every game has a “Play against the computer” mode with 3 levels.

Which languages?

English, Spanish and French. The app follows your device language, and you can switch on the home screen.

Which devices?

iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets. A tablet lying flat between two players is ideal.

What is the Evening Tournament?

A family challenge: one game a night for 7, 15 or 21 evenings, 2 to 4 players, with standings, a podium and medals. Names stay on the device.

What about the microphone?

It is only used in The Sound Factory, if a parent turns it on: the sound is analysed on the phone, nothing is recorded or sent.

Shall we play together tonight?

Free, no ads, no account. Download PapiDuo and put the phone between you.
