FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes: every game, with no ads and no purchases.

Do I need internet or an account?

No. PapiDuo works completely offline, with no sign-up. No data is collected.

What age is it for?

Ages 4 to 10, with an adult or another child. Every game has suitable levels.

What if we play alone?

Almost every game has a “Play against the computer” mode with 3 levels.

Which languages?

English, Spanish and French. The app follows your device language, and you can switch on the home screen.

Which devices?

iPhone and iPad. An iPad lying flat between two players is ideal.

What is the Evening Tournament?

A family challenge: one game a night for 7, 15 or 21 evenings, 2 to 4 players, with standings, a podium and medals. Names stay on the device.
